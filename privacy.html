Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

DUCK Browser uses Google Sign-In only to back up the user's own app data. It requests the scopes openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata.

  • The email address is shown in the app so the user knows which account is linked.
  • Backups are encrypted on the user's computer and stored only in the hidden app-data folder of the user's own Google Drive. The app cannot see any other Drive files.
  • No data is sent to the developer or any third party. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The user can sign out in the app at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; removing the app there deletes its app-data folder.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
